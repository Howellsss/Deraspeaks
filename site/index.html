About

The Oracle.

A Nigerian Christian spoken-word minister and songwriter, drawing people toward authentic faith and the reality of God.

Biography

Who is Dera Speaks?

Chidera Favour Ogbonnaya, professionally known as Dera Speaks, is a Nigerian Christian spoken-word minister and songwriter whose work uses words, music, worship, and performance to draw people toward authentic faith and the reality of God.

Her work is marked by a direct, emotionally honest approach to Christian expression. Through spoken word and music, she explores themes of gratitude, faith, identity, worship, salvation, restoration, and the inner life of the believer. Her voice has developed a distinctive place within a generation seeking a faith that is honest, expressive, and deeply rooted in Christ.

Described by the press as an unapologetic, “warrior-militant” minister, she treats ministry as a full-time calling that spans speaking, writing, singing and acting.

Her public journey began gaining attention from around 2018, and her work has since taken her from digital platforms to major ministry and performance spaces, including Winners Canaanland, Salvation Ministries Headquarters, Mountain of Fire and Miracles Headquarters, RCCG Headquarters, The Future Awards Africa, and international ministry in Kenya and the United Kingdom.

The ministry mandate & philosophy

Not entertainment alone. A vehicle for encounter.

Her purpose is to restore and elevate people's imagination spiritually, and create an atmosphere of calm, comfort, and belief in all circumstances.

Dera Speaks carries the Word of God with a voice that is direct, expressive, and difficult to ignore. Her spoken word is not presented as entertainment alone; it is a vehicle for reflection, worship, conviction, encouragement, and renewed spiritual imagination.

Her work often meets people in the places they rarely speak about: disappointment, isolation, personal struggle, questions of identity, and the tension between what they believe and what they are experiencing. Through words and sound, she points listeners back toward faith, grace, gratitude, and the reality of God.

Before you send

Common questions.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as possible. International and large-capacity events benefit from several months' notice so travel and logistics can be planned properly.

Where does Dera travel from?

Travel is usually planned from Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Include flight and accommodation needs in Block 3 of the form.

Is submitting the form a confirmation?

No. It is a request. The administrative team of Dera Speaks Studios reviews every invitation and follows up.

Can our event be recorded or streamed?

Tell the team in the event details. Recording and streaming are agreed in advance as part of the booking.
